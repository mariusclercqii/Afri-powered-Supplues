Some FAQ’s

Let the sunshine inside your house

What warranties come with the products?

Our product warranties vary depending on the specific item purchased. Solar panels typically come with extended performance guarantees, while inverters and other accessories may have shorter coverage periods. Please refer to the individual product details for exact warranty information

What is included with the purchase?

Included with your purchase is the product in its original packaging, containing all the essentials you need to get started. Please note, any additional accessories or components are sold separately

What are the payment methods?

We accept a wide range of payment methods for your convenience. This includes major credit and debit cards such as Visa, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover. For seamless transactions, ensure your card is enabled for online purchases

How fast I get my order?

After your payment is successfully processed, your order will be on its way to you, arriving within 2-3 working days. We prioritize prompt shipping to ensure you receive your purchase quickly and efficiently.

How can I recieve my order?

You can receive your order through two convenient options: opt for courier delivery to have it sent directly to your doorstep, or choose to pick it up at our shop address. Select your preferred method at checkout for a smooth and tailored shopping experience.
