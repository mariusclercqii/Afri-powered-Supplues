What warranties come with the products?

Our product warranties vary depending on the specific item purchased. Solar panels typically come with extended performance guarantees, while inverters and other accessories may have shorter coverage periods. Please refer to the individual product details for exact warranty information.

What is included with the purchase?

Included with your purchase is the product in its original packaging, containing all the essentials you need to get started. Any additional accessories or components are sold separately.

What are the payment methods?

Order on WhatsApp, or pay online with the Pay now button on a product.

How fast do I get my order?

After your payment is processed, your order will arrive within 2-3 working days.

How can I receive my order?

You can choose courier delivery to your doorstep, or pick up at our shop address.
